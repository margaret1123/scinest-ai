## What is SciNest?

**Turn your research materials into publication-ready artifacts with any AI model you choose.**

SciNest is a desktop workbench for students, researchers, and knowledge workers. You bring your API keys (OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, Gemini, GLM, Kimi, Mistral, Qwen, and more), select your project materials, and SciNest transforms them into:

- **Research drafts, proposals, and thesis sections** with citation-aware editing
- **Scientific figures and graphical abstracts** with editable overlays and layers
- **Slide decks** with 21 design templates and material-backed outlines
- **Reference lists** with source tracking and export

No prompt engineering needed. The app is the prompt.
